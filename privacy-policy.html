Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025  |  Effective immediately

Welcome to TubeSnap ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Gidsworld Ventures. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at tubesnap.io.

1. Information We Collect

TubeSnap does not require you to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information to use our service. We automatically collect limited technical data when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URLs. This data is collected in aggregate form and is used solely to improve our service.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and serve personalised advertisements. These include first-party cookies (set by TubeSnap) and third-party cookies from partners such as Google AdSense.

Google AdSense may use cookies such as the DART cookie to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to websites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. To learn more about how Google uses data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve TubeSnap, to understand how visitors interact with our site, and to display relevant advertisements. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties other than as described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain automatically collected usage data for a maximum of 12 months for analytical purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Children's Privacy

TubeSnap is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Policy
