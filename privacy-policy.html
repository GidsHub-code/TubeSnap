Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025  ·  Operated by Gidsworld Ventures

Welcome to TubeSnap (tubesnap.io). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how we handle any information related to your use of our website. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

TubeSnap does not require account creation or personal data submission. When you visit our site, we automatically collect limited technical data including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, and referring URLs. This data is collected in aggregate form for analytical purposes only and is never tied to your identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain TubeSnap, understand how visitors interact with our site, diagnose technical issues, and improve our service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and serve relevant advertising. These include first-party analytics cookies and third-party cookies from advertising partners including Google AdSense. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

TubeSnap uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other sites. The DART cookie allows Google to serve ads to users based on their visit history. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. For more information on how Google uses data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. YouTube URLs and Video Data

When you paste a YouTube URL into TubeSnap, we process that URL solely to retrieve publicly available video metadata (title, channel name, thumbnail) and provide download functionality. We do not store or log the video URLs you paste after your session ends.

6. Data Retention

Automatically collected technical and usage data is retained for a maximum of 12 months for analytical purposes, after which it is deleted or fully anonymised.

7. Children's Privacy

TubeSnap is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal data through our site, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data under applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR or CCPA). To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Links

TubeSnap may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent changes. Continued use of TubeSnap after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@tubesnap.io
