DMCA Copyright Policy

TubeSnap respects intellectual property rights  ·  Operated by Gidsworld Ventures

TubeSnap (tubesnap.io), operated by Gidsworld Ventures, respects the intellectual property rights of all copyright holders and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). This page explains our DMCA policy and how to submit a valid takedown notice.

Our Position on Copyright

TubeSnap does not host, store, cache, or distribute any video content. Our service provides a technical interface that allows users to access publicly available video streams from YouTube. We do not encourage, endorse, or facilitate copyright infringement. Users are solely responsible for ensuring their use of downloaded content is lawful.

How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe that content accessible through TubeSnap infringes your copyright, you may submit a written takedown notice to our designated agent. Your notice must include all of the following:

  • Your full legal name, email address, mailing address, and telephone number.
  • A clear description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • A description of the specific material you claim is infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the copyright owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Send your notice to our designated DMCA agent: dmca@tubesnap.io

Response Time

We will review all valid DMCA notices and respond within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 5–10 business days. Invalid or incomplete notices may be disregarded.

DMCA Counter-Notice

If you believe that content was removed or disabled in error, or that you have the right to use the content, you may submit a counter-notice to dmca@tubesnap.io with the required DMCA counter-notification elements as specified in 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).

Repeat Infringers

TubeSnap maintains a policy to address users who repeatedly infringe the copyrights of others. We reserve the right to restrict access to our service for users who engage in repeated infringement.
