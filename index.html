How to Download a YouTube Video with TubeSnap

New to TubeSnap? Follow these four simple steps and you'll have your video downloaded in under a minute — works on any device.

🎬1

Open YouTube

Go to YouTube and find the video or Short you want to save to your device.

🔗2

Copy the Link

Copy the video URL from your browser bar or tap Share → Copy Link in the YouTube app.

📋3

Paste & Snap It

Paste the link into the TubeSnap box above and click the ⚡ Snap It button.

⬇️4

Pick Format & Download

Choose 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p MP4, or MP3. Your file downloads straight to your device.

Step 01

Finding the YouTube Video URL

On desktop, look at the address bar at the top of your browser while watching the video. The URL looks like youtube.com/watch?v=XXXXXXX. Click it and press Ctrl+C (or ⌘+C on Mac) to copy. For YouTube Shorts, the URL will be youtube.com/shorts/XXXXXXX — copy it the same way.

📱 On iPhone or Android: tap the Share button under the video → tap Copy Link.
Step 02

Pasting the Link into TubeSnap

Tap the input box at the top of this page and press Ctrl+V to paste your link. Or tap the 📋 Paste button — TubeSnap will automatically read the link from your clipboard. Then click ⚡ Snap It to begin. TubeSnap will fetch the video title and thumbnail within seconds.

✅ All URL formats work: youtube.com/watch, youtu.be short links, and youtube.com/shorts links.
Step 03

Choosing Your Download Format

Once TubeSnap loads the video info you'll see the thumbnail, title, and channel name. Below that are five download buttons: 1080p HD, 720p HD, 480p, 360p (all MP4 video), and MP3 Audio. Click whichever quality best suits your needs and storage space.

🎵 Want only the sound? Click the gold MP3 Audio button to extract just the audio track.
Step 04

Saving the Downloaded File

After clicking a format button, TubeSnap converts the video — this takes 10–30 seconds depending on video length. Once ready, the download starts automatically. On desktop, find your file in the Downloads folder. On mobile, check your Files app or notification bar.

⚡ Best all-round pick: 720p MP4 — great quality, compatible with all devices, moderate file size.
